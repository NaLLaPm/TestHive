OUR MANIFESTO

Mindful Fashion for Everyday Living

Founded on the principle that exceptional clothing shouldn't be reserved for special occasions, THREAD & TREND crafts understated, high-quality wardrobe essentials designed to move effortlessly between professional and casual moments.

We believe in slow fashion values: choosing European washed flax linens, certified long-staple organic cottons, and durable Japanese stretch denim designed to last through seasons rather than fleeting micro-trends.

Our Core Philosophy

"Simplicity is not the lack of clutter, that's simply the consequence of simplicity. Simplicity somehow essentially describes the purpose and place of an object and its materials."

Each garment is designed in our Bengaluru design studio with meticulous attention to tailoring, French seams, natural horn buttons, and breathable natural fibers that celebrate modern lifestyle aesthetics.
