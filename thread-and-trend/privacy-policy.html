Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At THREAD & TREND, your privacy and personal data protection are paramount to our core operations. This policy describes how customer information is gathered, stored, and protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you place a demo order or browse our website, we may collect contact details (full name, email address, telephone number), delivery address, and shopping cart preferences.

2. Local Storage Usage

In this demonstration application, your shopping cart contents, saved wishlist identifiers, and demo order history are stored strictly on your local computer via browser LocalStorage. We do not transmit or sell this information to external marketing brokers.

3. Payment Card Data Security

We do not store complete 16-digit credit card numbers, CVVs, or card pins anywhere in local storage, session storage, or tracking logs. Real implementations use tokenized payment gateways (such as Razorpay, Stripe, or Cashfree) adhering to PCI-DSS Level 1 specifications.

4. Contact Inquiries

For data removal requests or queries regarding your browsing data, contact us at privacy@threadandtrend.demo.
